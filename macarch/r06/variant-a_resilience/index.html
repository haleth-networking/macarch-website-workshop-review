Field record

Tested by the real world.

Our earth-bermed, ferrocement buildings have been through hurricanes and earthquakes in the Caribbean and the US — with no loss of life and no structural damage. Every one came through Hurricanes Ivan and Sandy. Our Jamaica villas have since stood through Hurricanes Dean, Beryl and Melissa, and a magnitude 7.7 Caribbean earthquake.

Otoe Casino, in Red Rock, Oklahoma since 2000, has been through the 2016 Pawnee M5.8 earthquake. Our Fort Myers home has stood through Hurricane Ian (2022).

Three threats, one structure

Designed for the worst day, and every day after

Storm & flood

Monolithic structure with no weak joints for wind and surge to exploit. Built at or above flood-resistant standards; one of our homes is Corps of Engineers certified.

Fire

Non-combustible shells and roofs, with no exposed timber and no ember traps.

Seismic

Steel-reinforced shells that flex without collapse.

Security & EMP

Hardened envelopes, safe rooms, protected power and water, and systems that keep running when the grid does not.

“Earthquakes by themselves kill very few people. The weight of falling concrete and masonry fill kills people.”

— Angus W. Macdonald, Ferro-7

Earth-sheltered & passive solar

Earth is the best insulation money cannot buy. Bermed and sheltered homes hold a steady temperature year-round, and paired with passive solar gain they can run on little or no purchased energy.
