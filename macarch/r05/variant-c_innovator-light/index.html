Field record

Tested by the real world.

Our earth-bermed, ferrocement buildings have been through hurricanes and earthquakes in the Caribbean and the US — with no loss of life and no structural damage. Every one came through Hurricanes Ivan and Sandy. Our Jamaica villas have since stood through Hurricanes Dean, Beryl and Melissa, and a magnitude 7.7 Caribbean earthquake.

Otoe Casino, in Red Rock, Oklahoma since 2000, has been through the 2016 Pawnee M5.8 earthquake. Our Fort Myers home has stood through Hurricane Ian (2022).

The system

One seamless shell

Galvanized steel profiles and a galvanized steel matrix, encased in high-strength mortar, form a seamless unibody shell: foundation, walls and roof as one structure. With no weak joints, there is nothing for wind, water or seismic force to pry apart. Panels are prefabricated under factory control and assembled on site.

The system is manufactured by our sister company, am-cor inc. We design with am-cor where it serves the project, and with conventional systems where they serve it better. am-cor.com →
